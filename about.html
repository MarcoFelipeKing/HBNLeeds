Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

A world where all buildings enhance the health, well-being, and sustainability of their occupants and the planet through collaborative, evidence-based approaches.

Our Mission

Healthy Buildings Network Leeds emerged to foster meaningful collaboration on how buildings impact health. We actively engage across campuses, councils, and industries—driving innovative research for healthier indoor environments.
