Frequently Asked Questions

How can I get involved with the Healthy Buildings Network?

There are several ways to get involved with our network:

  • Become a member to receive updates and invitations to events
  • Attend our events including seminars, workshops, and conferences
  • Collaborate on research projects or propose new collaborations
  • Share your expertise as a speaker at one of our events
Is membership in the network free?

Yes, membership in the Healthy Buildings Network Leeds is completely free. We believe in open collaboration and making our resources and knowledge accessible to all interested parties.

Can I propose a research collaboration?

Absolutely! We welcome research collaboration proposals from academics, industry professionals, and community organizations. Please contact us with details about your proposal, and one of our directors will get back to you to discuss potential collaborations.

When is the next funding round for research projects?

We have now completed two rounds of pump-priming and ECR-led research funding, supporting eight projects across the network. Details of any future funding opportunities will be announced via our mailing list and the Projects page.

Healthy Buildings Network Leeds is a cross-disciplinary research and collaboration hub at the University of Leeds, focused on healthier, more sustainable built environments.
