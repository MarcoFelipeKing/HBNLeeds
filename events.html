Seminar

From Field to Foundation: Natural Buildings in Yorkshire

May 28, 2025
12:00 PM - 1:30 PM
Clarendon 2.08 / MS Teams

"Bridging agriculture and construction: Sustainable materials for healthier indoor environments."

Join us for an insightful seminar with Dr. Gesa Reiss, Senior Innovation Fellow at the University of Leeds. With her extensive experience in agri-food sustainability and as former Low Carbon Economy Development Officer, Dr. Reiss will explore how Yorkshire's natural materials can revolutionize construction practices while creating healthier living spaces.

You'll learn about:

  • Natural building materials and their impact on indoor air quality
  • Circular economy principles connecting agriculture and construction
  • Yorkshire's unique contributions to sustainable building practices
