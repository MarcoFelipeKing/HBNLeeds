Hybrid Seminar

Indoor Air Pollution and Cardiac Health

with Dr. Jacobo Elies

February 12, 2026
12:00 PM - 1:00 PM
Worsley 8.11A / MS Teams

"We know air pollution causes arrhythmias. Now we're uncovering why."

Join Dr. Jacobo Elies, Associate Professor at the University of Bradford, as he explores the cellular mechanisms linking indoor air pollutants to cardiac dysfunction. Using cutting-edge electrophysiological and proteomic approaches, this seminar reveals how common domestic pollutants like carbon monoxide alter cardiac ion channel function and disrupt normal heart rhythm.

You'll learn about:

  • How gaseous air pollutants affect cardiac ion channels
  • The mechanisms behind pollution-induced arrhythmias
  • Genetic factors influencing individual susceptibility
