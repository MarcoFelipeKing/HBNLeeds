About the Event

The Healthy Buildings Network (HBN) invites all members, affiliated researchers, and partners to its annual Showcase Event. This is an opportunity to celebrate and explore the interdisciplinary research we have funded, hear from leading voices in healthy-building science, and shape future directions for the network.

The day features:

  • Keynote addresses from leading researchers
  • Project presentations from our 4 Pump-Priming and 4 Early Career Researcher grant holders
  • Panel discussion on future priorities for healthy buildings research
  • Networking across disciplines, faculties, and external partners

The event is open to all HBN members and affiliated individuals. Whether you are an existing collaborator or interested in joining the network, you are very welcome.
