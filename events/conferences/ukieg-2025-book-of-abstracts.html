About this book

The UK Indoor Environments Group holds an annual conference bringing together researchers and practitioners working on indoor environmental quality. The 2025 meeting was hosted at the University of Leeds by the Healthy Buildings Network and asked what the transition to net zero means for health indoors.

This volume collects the abstracts presented on the day. It is published with an ISBN, so individual abstracts and the volume as a whole can be cited.
