# LinkedIn post: World Ventilation Day Round Table 2026 (post-event)

Written for Marco-Felipe King to post from his own account. Tag the networks and
speakers where you can. No em dashes, to match how the site reads.

---

Forty per cent of the room was undecided.

Not the general public. A room of sixty building physicists, aerosol scientists,
environmental health researchers and housing practitioners, on World Ventilation Day,
voting on this:

"This house believes the UK should set a legally binding indoor air quality limit now,
before the evidence is complete."

Five networks sent one champion each, with three minutes and one slide. No keynotes,
no panel platitudes, and a cross-examination round where they had to challenge each
other directly.

What came out of it was sharper than I expected:

→ Doug Booker (HESTIA): airtight retrofit without verified ventilation is a health
intervention nobody consented to.

→ Abigail Hathway (Air Hub): we have forgotten why we moved indoors in the first place.
Stop telling people to open windows when the problem is sources, or when it is 30 degrees
outside.

→ Bruño Fraga (BreatHE IN): 300,000 CO2 monitors in classrooms and 95% of the advice is
still "open a window". Measurement with no duty to act is theatre.

→ Marcella Ucci (UKIEG): while we argue about what a good enough limit would be, we are
quietly accepting that high levels are fine in the meantime.

→ Irene Mussio (HBN): ventilation loses every budget fight because we report it in litres
per second instead of illness prevented and cost avoided.

Then we voted again. Yes went up six points. No went up nine. Undecided dropped fifteen.
The room moved in both directions, which tells you something about how good the arguments
on each side were. (Smaller sample at the end, so read it as indicative.)

The part I care most about comes next. Each of the five networks is writing a commitment
for what it will actually deliver by World Ventilation Day 2027. Those pledges are still
coming in. When they land, all five go up side by side on our site, unedited, so anyone
can hold us to them next November.

That is the bit that turns a good hour into something that counts.

Full write-up, including the cross-examination round and the questions from the floor:
https://www.healthybuildingsnetwork.org/blog/world-ventilation-day-2026.html

Thanks to everyone who came, voted, and argued well.
