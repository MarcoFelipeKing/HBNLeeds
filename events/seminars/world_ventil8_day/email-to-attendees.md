Dear all,

Thank you for joining the World Ventilation Day Round Table last Friday. Sixty of you
voted, the chat never stopped, and the hour went faster than any of us expected.

A reminder of where the room landed. Before anyone spoke, 38% backed the motion that the
UK should set a legally binding indoor air quality limit now, 22% opposed it, and 40%
were undecided. In the closing poll, Yes rose to 44% and No to 31%, with Undecided down
to 25%. Far fewer people caught the closing vote than the opening one, so treat that
shift as indicative rather than conclusive. But the room moved, and it moved in both
directions, which says something about how good the arguments were on each side.

Three things are now online.

1. The write-up
All five provocations, the cross-examination round where the speakers challenged each
other, and the discussion from the floor.
https://www.healthybuildingsnetwork.org/blog/world-ventilation-day-2026.html

2. Your questions, answered
We ran out of time for four questions in the chat. All four have now been answered in
writing by the panellists, including why CO2 is a poor proxy for infection risk and what
might work better instead, and whether we lean too hard on mechanical ventilation in
airtight buildings.
https://www.healthybuildingsnetwork.org/events/seminars/world_ventil8_day/world_ventil8_day.html#questions

3. Five commitments for 2027
Each network said what it would do before the next World Ventilation Day. All five are
now published, unedited, side by side.
https://www.healthybuildingsnetwork.org/events/seminars/world_ventil8_day/world_ventil8_day.html#pledges

We will bring the same five networks back together on World Ventilation Day 2027 to
report on whether we actually did any of it. That was our own commitment, so please do
hold us to it.
