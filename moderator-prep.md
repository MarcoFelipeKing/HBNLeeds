## HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT

- **Opening round:** Each panellist has been assigned one of the 8 curated questions below.
  Introduce it, briefly attribute it ("this came from several attendees"), then pass to the assigned panellist.
- **Audience follow-ups:** Raw responses in each theme cluster give you language and angles for probe questions.
- **Floor questions:** Remind audience at the end that they submitted 30 questions and you've covered 8 — invite floor contributions on anything not yet covered.
- **Time check:** Panel session is 90 minutes (14:15–15:45). Roughly 8 min per theme, with 6 min spare for overflow.

## THEME 4 · EVERYDAY AIR QUALITY
**Assigned panellist: Dr Douglas Booker (HESTIA)**

### Curated question
> *How should a typical householder — without specialist measuring tools — know when their indoor air is worse than outdoors? What does genuinely useful, consistent public messaging around ventilation and air quality look like?*

### Raw submissions in this cluster
- "Any research on what typical levels of CO2 and other pollutants in a wide range of UK homes, throughout the day and night, and throughout the year?"
- "How to provide households with consistent messaging about need of ventilation & air quality and maintaining balance with heating costs, security, external noise etc?"
- "How should a typical householder (without a range of measuring tools available), know when outside air pollution is worse than indoor air pollution, ie should they open a window or not?"
- "Monitoring the effectiveness of ventilation in domestic buildings, particularly in existing buildings where this has been retrofitted. For example the UK Building Regulations ventilation requirements of dry room trickle vents and wet room mechanical extract ventilation. How well ventilated are houses that have compliant systems? How do airtightness levels affect this?"
- "Is moisture load the most practical ventilation control metric, considering affordability? What should we be looking to monitor in the future? How realistic is this assuming this is not moisture, but perhaps CO2 or a wider range of pollutant measures?"
- "How well do MVHR mini systems work? Are there conditions in which they work better? These may be the push-pull ones, or more sophisticated ones like the bluMartin FreeAir 100."

### Probe questions for moderator
- "Do compliant ventilation systems actually deliver adequate ventilation in practice?"
- "Is CO₂ the most useful proxy for air quality that a householder could act on?"

## THEME 5 · AFFORDABILITY & RETROFIT

### Curated question

### Raw submissions in this cluster

## THEME 7 · PEOPLE & PLANET
**Assigned panellist: Prof. Marcella Ucci (UKIEG)**

### Curated question
> *How can we reconcile the competing demands of energy efficiency, climate resilience, and indoor air quality in future building standards and retrofits — and make buildings that are genuinely healthy for people AND the planet?*

### Raw submissions in this cluster
- "How can we make buildings that are healthy for people AND the planet?"
- "How can we reconcile the often-competing demands of energy efficiency, climate resilience, and superior indoor air quality in future building standards and retrofits?"
- "What breakthroughs in ventilation strategies, material science, or smart systems are needed to prevent issues like condensation on dry-lined walls, poor airflow behind kitchen units, and pollutant buildup — while keeping buildings affordable and low-carbon?"
- "Is it energy intensive to incorporate smart and adaptive technology to healthy buildings?"
- "How can use visable light (soret band) help protect people within their homes? The use of visible light (soret band) on controlling mould spores within the home? The use of visible light (soret band) to control the microbial load within the home?" *(niche/emerging — may be better as a floor question)*
- "Are we being 'chemically castrated' unintentionally by the chemicals and toxins in our home and environment? How do we 'red pill' the general public to wake up to the toxins we are continuously exposed to in our environment?" *(flag: emotive framing — rephrase if raised from floor as "what are we still not measuring for exposure to synthetic chemicals in home environments?")*
- "How many building products contain PFAs and their impact on internal environments and health?"

### Probe questions for moderator
- "Is there a documented case where energy efficiency improvements have measurably worsened indoor air quality?"
- "PFAS in building products — is this a research gap or a policy gap?"

## THEME 8 · TECHNOLOGY ADOPTION
**Assigned panellist: Dr Bruño Fraga (BREATHEIN)**

### Curated question
> *Real-time IAQ monitoring, smart ventilation, and AI-driven building controls already exist. What needs to happen — across industry, policy, and research — to accelerate their adoption in homes and buildings?*

### Raw submissions in this cluster
- "Many promising technologies — such as real-time IAQ monitoring, smart ventilation, and AI-driven building controls — already exist. What needs to happen across industry, policy, and research to accelerate their widespread adoption in homes and buildings?"
- "Looking ahead to 2030 and beyond, what role should advanced technologies (such as AI-driven building controls, biophilic design integration, improved filtration, and continuous IAQ sensors) play in creating adaptive, resilient indoor environments — and what gaps in current research must be addressed to make truly 'healthy-by-default' buildings the norm rather than the exception?"
- "What are the most promising emerging research areas for understanding and mitigating the long-term health impacts of indoor air pollutants (including VOCs, particulates, pathogens, and newer concerns like microplastics or wildfire smoke infiltration), and how can we translate these findings into measurable performance standards for healthy buildings?"
